About The Oak at Dewlish

A traditionally run Dorset village inn

The Oak at Dewlish is situated in the heart of Hardy Country. As a free house, it offers a good range of guest beers from the local area and serves a high standard of traditional home-cooked pub food in a friendly, informal atmosphere.

Whether you're joining us for a meal, a quiet drink by the fire, or a comfortable stay in our Coach House or B&B rooms, we look forward to welcoming you.
